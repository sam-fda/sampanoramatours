Optimise for web

Your 16384×8192 PNG is ~88 MB and won't render on many phones. This exports lighter JPG copies and points the tour at them. Put the downloaded files in panos/.

Publish

Many tours can share one folder: give each a Tour ID (→ <id>.js), upload them all next to viewer.html, and each QR opens its own via ?tour=<id>. Blank ID = the default config.js.
